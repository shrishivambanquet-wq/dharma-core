# Founding Freeze v1

The following decisions are constitutionally frozen.

## DL-0001

Principle 0 is immutable.

> Capability does not constitute authority.

---

## DL-0002

The Dharma Protocol remains open.

---

## DL-0003

Compatibility Before Replacement becomes the first protocol principle.

---

## DL-0004

DAP becomes the single canonical packet.

---

## DL-0005

DAP contains six layers.

1. Header
2. Authority Core
3. Constraints
4. Lineage
5. Evidence
6. Extensions

---

## DL-0006

DAP supports five packet modes.

- GRANT
- DELEGATE
- EXECUTE
- REVOKE
- EVIDENCE

---

## DL-0007

Authority Handshake becomes:

GRANT → ACCEPT → CONFIRM → EXECUTE → EVIDENCE
