# Preamble

Human civilization built laws for people, contracts for organizations, and protocols for computers.

Dharma proposes a constitutional layer that governs legitimate authority across humans, AI agents, enterprises, payment systems, APIs, and future autonomous systems.

This Constitution defines the principles from which every future Dharma specification MUST derive.

---

# Founder's Oath

I, Paresh Somani, establish Dharma with the belief that power without legitimate authority creates instability.

I will preserve human intent before machine capability and ensure every future implementation remains faithful to Principle 0.

---

# Principle 0

**Capability does not constitute authority.**

A system MAY possess the ability to perform an action.

That ability NEVER becomes legitimate authority by itself.

This principle is immutable.

---

# Article I — Nature of Authority

Authority is distinct from identity.

Every authority SHOULD contain:

- Provenance
- Legitimacy
- Purpose
- Scope
- Lifetime

Authority MUST remain traceable throughout its lifecycle.

---

# Article II — Delegation

Delegation creates lineage.

Every delegated authority MUST preserve ancestry.

A child authority MUST NOT exceed its parent.

---

# Article III — Constraints

Authority carries its own constraints.

Examples include:

- Money
- Time
- Geography
- Organization
- Risk
- Purpose

Constraints travel together with authority.

---

# Article IV — Intent

Intent explains why an action exists.

Inference MAY recommend.

Authority decides.

---

# Article V — Effects

Dharma evaluates the final real-world effect rather than only the initial request.

---

# Article VI — Revocation

Authority MUST remain revocable.

Descendant authorities SHOULD respect revocation rules.

---

# Article VII — Evidence

Every consequential action SHOULD produce verifiable evidence preserving:

- Issuer
- Subject
- Action
- Effect
- Timestamp

---

# Article VIII — Identity

Identity answers:

> Who is acting?

Authority answers:

> What may legitimately happen?

These concepts MUST remain separate.

---

# Article IX — Conflict

Future constitutional procedures MAY define conflict resolution between users, enterprises, and regulators.

---

# Article X — Universality

Dharma is protocol-agnostic.

It is designed to interoperate with:

- HTTP
- MCP
- A2A
- Payment Systems
- Enterprise Platforms
- Robotics

without replacing them.
